Travel Essentials

Things every traveler should remember.

🎒 Carry With You

  • Warm Clothes
  • Power Bank
  • Identity Proof
  • Medicines
  • Trekking Shoes
  • Water Bottle

⚠ Avoid

  • Littering
  • Plastic Waste
  • Dangerous Trekking Alone
  • Feeding Wildlife
  • Ignoring Weather Alerts
  • Driving Rashly on Hills
